Mathematical Model Breakdown

The system operates in a strict temporal domain of [7 days, 2579 days] (1 week to 7 years), with a total range of 2572 days. The punishing force is capped at an absolute maximum of 63.75%.

1. Conviction Weight ($w_i$)

Ignores generic votes and grants power to precise votes. A vote covering the entire term (total indifference) has a weight of 0.

Formula
w_i = 1 - (b_i - a_i) / 2572

2. Center of Gravity & Lower Anchor

The midpoint of each vote ($m_i$) is used. We calculate the global mean of the population ($T_0$) and the exclusive mean of the "punishing" group ($T_{low}$), which acts as the gravitational anchor.

Global Mean ($T_0$)
T_0 = Σ (w_i * m_i) / Σ w_i
Punishing Group Anchor ($T_{low}$)
T_{low} = Σ_{m_i < T_0} (w_i * m_i) / Σ_{m_i < T_0} w_i

3. Continuous Quadratic Dispersion Index ($S^2$)

Calculates how divided the community is relative to the center. It is squared to forgive slight variations and heavily punish only deep social fractures.

Dispersion and Normalization
σ = √ [ Σ (w_i * (m_i - T_0)²) / Σ w_i ]
S = σ / 1286

4. Attraction Force & Final Result ($T$)

The punishing force is a combination of the arbitrary cap (63.75%) and the quadratic dispersion index ($S^2$).

Final Result
W = 0.6375 * S²

T = (1 - W) * T_0 + W * T_{low}

The F Constant and the 7-Year Limit

Initially, the formula had an arbitrary penalty factor F (0 to 10) and a variable term limit X. We wanted to guarantee that, in the worst-case scenario of radical polarization (30% maximum and 30% minimum), the result $p$ would be exactly 1 year and 3 months (473 days).

The math showed that all variables were intertwined. For the minimum to be 7 days, the maximum limit ($X$) to be elegant (7 years = 2579 days), and the extreme punishment to result in ~473 days, the F factor had to be fixed at 8.5 (which equates to a 63.75% maximum gravitational force).
